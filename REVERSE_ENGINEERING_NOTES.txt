ChittiSat reverse-engineering notes
================================
- Windows executable: Flutter desktop app.
- Flutter engine: flutter_windows.dll
- Compiled Dart: data/app.so
- Serial dependencies: flutter_libserialport_plugin.dll + serialport.dll
- Network strings found: https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast and http://ip-api.com/json
- Dart model/class strings include SatelliteData, MovementData, WeatherData, PollutionData,
  SerialPortSatelliteDataRepo, SerialPortReader, SerialPortConfig, DashboardPage,
  WeatherPanel, MovementPanel, PollutionPanel, SatelliteWidget.
- Original bundled satellite assets: assets/models/satellite.obj/.mtl
- Original weather assets included.
- Screenshot confirms a dark landscape dashboard with Weather, Movement, Air Quality,
  altitude gauge, serial device status, port selector, and data status.
